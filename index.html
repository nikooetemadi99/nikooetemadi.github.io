About

I recently completed my Master’s in Aerospace Engineering at Carleton University, where I specialized in rotorcraft stability, structural dynamics and simulation-based modelling. My graduate research, carried out in collaboration with Bombardier, focused on helicopter and propeller-rotor stability and on understanding — and mitigating — whirl flutter using MATLAB and Maple.

Today my focus is mechanical design: parametric 3D modelling and assemblies in CATIA V5 and Fusion 360, production drawings with GD&T, and design-for-manufacturability backed by analysis in ANSYS and MATLAB. Hands-on time in aircraft maintenance at IR Airline, embedded-systems research at Akimbo Technologies, and years of teaching CAD have taught me to design for the person who has to make, inspect and maintain the part.

I’m looking to contribute to aerospace or mechanical projects where systems thinking, careful modelling and collaborative problem-solving matter.
